# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- Wider dropdowns on the settings page, without the arrows beside them

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-24

### Added

- `Per character` checkbox and `/sbg character on|off`: settings per account or per character
- `/sbg test`, `/sbg reset`, and `/sbg debug on|off`

### Fixed

- Stutter from re-placing items on every layout, show, and hide; bursts now apply once
- Re-placing protected viewers in combat now waits until combat ends

## [0.1.4] - 2026-11-24

### Changed

- Addon icon is now Gift of the Wild

## [0.1.3] - 2026-11-21

## [0.1.2] - 2026-11-21

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-21

### Added

- Close gaps between shown tracked buffs (icons and bars) when `Hide when inactive` is on
- Choose the growth direction of buff bars (down or up)
- Options panel below the Edit Mode dialog of Tracked Buffs and Tracked Bars
- Hide tracked totems the game leaves on screen after they are gone, out of combat
